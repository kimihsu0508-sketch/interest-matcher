# Interest Matcher

Welcome to the Interest Matcher platform! This application helps you find people to engage in various activities such as:

- **Playing Sports**: Connect with others who share your passion for sports and enjoy playing together.
- **Practicing English**: Meet individuals looking to practice their English language skills in an interactive way.
- **Working on Projects**: Collaborate with like-minded people to work on projects that interest you.

### Features
- Match with users based on shared interests.
- Easy-to-use interface for finding partners for sports or projects.
- Options for messaging and arranging meetups.

### Getting Started
1. Sign up for an account.
2. Complete your profile with your interests.
3. Start finding matches!
